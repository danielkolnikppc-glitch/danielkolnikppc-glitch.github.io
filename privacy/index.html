Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Daniel Kolník, a freelance PPC and analytics consultant based in Slovakia, handles data in Daniel Kolnik Reporting. It is an internal tool used only by me to work with the advertising and analytics accounts of my clients. It is not offered to the public.

Data the tool accesses

After I sign in with my own Google account, the tool can access data that account is authorized to see:

  • Google Ads: account structure, campaign settings and performance data
  • Google Analytics: property configuration and reports
  • Google Search Console: site, sitemap and search performance data

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the agreed services to the client it belongs to, such as reporting, audits, tracking checks and campaign management. It is not used for advertising to individuals and is not used to build profiles of people.

Sharing and service providers

I do not sell this data. I run the tool locally with Claude, an AI assistant provided by Anthropic, so the data returned for a request is processed by Anthropic as a service provider. Otherwise, data is shared only with the client it belongs to, or where the law requires it.

Storage and security

Google sign-in credentials are stored locally on my computer with restricted file permissions. Access to client accounts is limited to me and is removed when the cooperation ends. Reports and working notes are kept only as long as needed for the cooperation or to meet legal obligations.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

Clients can remove my access in their Google accounts at any time. For questions about your data, or to exercise your rights under the GDPR, contact me at danielkolnik.ppc@gmail.com.
